Emergency faucet repair: when a drip becomes a flood

Faucet repair in Coachella, CA covers cartridges, washers, O-rings, aerators, supply lines and shutoff valves, plus full replacement when a fixture is worn out. Most faucet problems are slow, but a burst supply line or a handle that will not shut off can flood a cabinet fast. Our 24/7 line is open at night and on weekends for those cases.

While you wait, close the small angle stop under the sink by turning it clockwise. If it will not turn or leaks, shut off the main water valve for the house. Move items out of the cabinet and put a towel or pan under the leak.

What your faucet is telling you

Drip from the spout

A worn cartridge, washer or seat. The part is cheap, and the waste adds up over weeks.

Water at the base of the handle

Failed O-rings or a loose packing nut.

Low or uneven flow

Mineral buildup in the aerator or a partly closed stop valve.

Wet cabinet floor

A cracked supply line or a leaking connection, which should be checked right away.

Handle that is hard to turn

Scale inside the cartridge, common where water is hard.

Repair or replace?

If the faucet is a quality fixture with a standard cartridge, repair is usually the better value. If the finish is pitted, the body is cracked, or parts are no longer made, replacement makes sense. We tell you which applies after looking at it, and we can install a fixture you bought yourself.

Bring up the shutoff valves while we are there. Old multi-turn stops often seize or weep when touched. Replacing them at the same visit means the next repair is a two-minute job rather than a house-wide shutdown.

Hard water and fixtures in the valley

Mineral content in the Coachella Valley's groundwater shows up as white crust on spouts and stiff cartridges. The Coachella Valley Water District reports hardness figures in its annual water quality report, and they vary by service area, so your own number may differ from a neighbor's.

If your aerators clog every few months, scale is the reason. Soaking an aerator in vinegar helps, and a whole-house option is covered on our water softener and filtration page.

Source type: Coachella Valley Water District 2025 water quality report.
