Emergency sewer line cleaning: what to do first

Sewer line cleaning in Coachella, CA clears the main drain that carries all household wastewater to the street, using cable machines and, for heavy buildup, hydro jetting. A blocked main sends sewage into the lowest fixtures first, usually a floor drain, shower or tub. That is an emergency. Our 24/7 line is open at any hour and a plumber can be scheduled the same day when openings allow.

Stop using water, including toilets, washers and dishwashers. If you can find the outdoor cleanout, do not open it yourself because sewage may flow out. Tell the dispatcher where the cleanout is, if you know.

How a main line clog shows up

Multiple fixtures slow or backed up

Toilets, tubs and floor drains clog together.

Gurgling

Air is being forced through traps by water trying to pass a blockage.

Backup at the lowest drain

Wastewater finds the lowest opening first.

Sewage odor outside

A blocked line can push gas and liquid through the cleanout.

Cleaning methods and what follows

A cable machine with a cutting head clears paper, roots and soft blockages through the cleanout. Heavy grease and scale need hydro jetting. After clearing, a camera run is worthwhile if the line has clogged before, because the cause may be a belly or crack that needs a sewer line repair.

We do not recommend repeated cleaning as a substitute for diagnosis. Two clogs within a short time mean the line deserves a look.

Sewer service in Coachella

The City of Coachella's Utilities Department operates sanitary sewer service and lists an emergency standby line for sewer overflows. Its office is at 53462 Enterprise Way and is open Monday through Thursday. Outside those hours, the standby line is the number to use for City main problems.

Some east valley communities have septic systems rather than sewer. If your property has a septic tank, a main line clean is a different job, so say so when you call.
