Emergency garbage disposal repair: sink backed up or leaking

Garbage disposal repair in Coachella, CA means clearing jams, resetting overloaded motors, replacing leaking seals and flanges, and installing a new unit when the old one is done. A disposal that leaks under the sink or backs water into both basins is an urgent problem, because kitchen waste water and food debris sit in the cabinet. Our 24/7 line is open at night and on weekends.

Turn off the disposal switch and unplug it or shut off its breaker before reaching near it. Never put your hand inside. Stop running the sink and dishwasher until a plumber checks the drain.

Common disposal problems

Humming but not spinning

A jam. The motor is trying to turn but the plates are stuck.

Nothing happens when switched on

A tripped reset button, a dead outlet or a failed motor.

Leak from the bottom

A cracked housing or failed seal, which usually means replacement.

Leak at the top ring

A loose or worn sink flange gasket.

Water in the other basin

A clogged drain line past the disposal. See drain cleaning.

Repair, replace or leave it out

A jam is a quick fix. A leaking housing is not repairable. If your disposal is more than ten years old, replacement is worth pricing, since new units run quieter and have stronger motors. Some households choose to remove the disposal altogether, and we can cap and restore the drain.

A plumber who installs a disposal should also check the dishwasher drain connection, the trap arm and the cabinet for earlier leaks. Many 'disposal problems' are slow drains in disguise.

Kitchen waste and local drains

Grease is the most common cause of kitchen drain trouble, and a disposal does not fix it. Grease cools in the pipe and builds a coating. If your home has older cast iron or clay drain lines, they scale and narrow, so a disposal can overload them sooner.

The City of Coachella Utilities Department operates the sewer. Anything that ends up in your line eventually reaches its system, so keep fats, oils and fibrous scraps out of the disposal.

What to do before we arrive

These steps limit damage and keep you safe until a plumber arrives.

  • Switch the disposal off at the wall switch and at the breaker
  • Never reach into the disposal, even when it is off. Use tongs to remove an object
  • Stop running the sink and dishwasher so the cabinet stays dry
  • Keep children and pets away from the cabinet and any standing water
  • Do not pour chemical drain cleaner into a backed-up sink
  • Tell the dispatcher whether water is leaking, backing up or both
