Tankless water heater service in Coachella, CA

A tankless water heater heats water as it flows through a heat exchanger, so there is no storage tank to run out. It is a good fit for homes that want continuous hot water and wall space. The trade-off is that tankless units are sensitive to flow, venting, gas supply and mineral scale, so installation and maintenance matter more than they do for a tank.

This page covers three jobs: new installation, repair of faults and error codes, and routine flush and descale. If your tank unit is failing, see water heater replacement to compare.

Flush and descale: the maintenance that decides lifespan

Hard water leaves scale inside the heat exchanger, which narrows passages, raises temperatures and triggers error codes. A flush circulates a descaling solution through the unit with a small pump and then rinses it clean. Many manufacturers call for flushing on a schedule, and the right interval depends on your water and usage.

Ask your water utility for its hardness figure. For the Coachella Valley Water District, the 2025 report lists hardness for its Cove Communities service area between 8.4 and 310 mg/L, averaging 120 mg/L, which is a wide range. If you do not know your number, a cheap test strip tells you and helps set the flush schedule.

Common faults and what they point to

Cold water sandwich or lukewarm output

Flow too low to fire the burner, a clogged inlet filter or scale on the exchanger.

Error code and shutdown

Ignition, venting, condensate or temperature faults. The code lists the system but not the root cause.

Water temperature swings

Low flow fixtures, a recirculation setup or sensor issues.

Condensate drain backing up

A blocked trap or neutralizer, common on high efficiency gas units.

What desert homes should know

Gas line capacity is the most missed detail. A tankless unit can draw several times the gas of a tank heater, so an old line sized for a tank may need upgrading. Venting also changes, and your city's inspector will care about clearances.

Coachella's Building Division handles plumbing permits and is open Monday through Thursday. If you are converting from a tank, ask about permit requirements before work begins. Plumbing work in California is checked against the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). The same code governs venting and gas connections, so we check clearances and gas sizing before we quote.

Source type: Coachella Valley Water District 2025 water quality report and City of Coachella Building Division page.

What to do before we arrive

These steps limit damage and keep you safe until a plumber arrives.

  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 and the gas utility before you call us
  • Do not open the vent or the gas cabinet yourself
  • Close the gas valve to the unit if you can reach it safely
  • If water is leaking near the unit, turn off its power and water valves
  • Do not reset the unit again and again, since repeated attempts can hide the original fault
  • Write down the error code and the model number for the dispatcher
