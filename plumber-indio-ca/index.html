Water in Indio: who supplies it and why it matters

Most of Indio is served by the Indio Water Authority, a joint powers authority formed in 2000 to deliver water to the City. Its service area covers roughly 38 square miles, and it pumps from groundwater wells. Because the source is groundwater, minerals build up as scale inside heaters and fixtures over time.

That is why we recommend flushing a tank heater yearly and checking aerators when flow drops. If you are not sure whether your address is on the Authority, call it at (760) 391-4038. Your provider decides which water quality report applies to you, and that report is the right basis for any softener decision.

Source type: Indio Water Authority 'About Us' page.

Festival weekends and rental properties

The Empire Polo Club in Indio hosts large festivals each spring, and traffic on Highway 111 and nearby streets slows to a crawl on those weekends. Many owners rent their homes short term to visitors, and a failed water heater or clogged drain on a changeover day is a real problem.

If you manage a rental, give us the address, the shutoff location and a contact who can approve a quote. We can then move quickly when a guest calls at night. Allow extra time for travel when a festival is on, and tell the dispatcher about road closures you know of.

Bermuda Dunes and the rest of the Indio area

Bermuda Dunes, an unincorporated community next door, shares Indio's 92203 ZIP and is part of our dispatch area. Homes there include golf-course properties and older ranch-style houses. The same rules apply as in Indio: call the 24/7 line, shut off the water at the main and tell us your street.

For sewer problems, tell us whether you are on a City line or a different system. Utilities and permit offices differ between Indio and nearby unincorporated areas, and we confirm yours before booking.
