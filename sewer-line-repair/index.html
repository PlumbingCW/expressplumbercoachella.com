Emergency sewer line repair after a backup

Sewer line repair in Coachella, CA is the work of fixing a damaged section of the pipe that carries wastewater from your home to the City main, using spot repair, trenchless lining or full replacement. A backup into tubs and floor drains is a health hazard and cannot wait. Our 24/7 line is open at night and on weekends, and a technician can usually be scheduled the same day when openings allow.

While you wait, stop using water and keep everyone out of affected areas. Avoid contact with sewage and wash hands thoroughly if you touch it. If your neighbors are affected too, call the City sewer standby line first.

Signs your sewer line needs repair, not just cleaning

  • Backups that return soon after snaking
  • Gurgling toilets and slow drains throughout the house
  • A sewage smell in the yard or garage
  • Wet or sunken ground over the sewer path
  • Cracks, offsets or roots visible on a camera video

Repair options and when each fits

Spot repair digs to the damaged section and replaces it. Pipe lining inserts a resin-coated liner inside the existing pipe, which avoids trenching. Full replacement removes the old pipe and installs new. A camera shows which applies. Lining suits pipes that are cracked but still aligned, and replacement suits collapsed, severely offset or bellied pipes. Contractors sometimes sell lining where it will not hold, so ask to see the video.

Before the repair, a camera inspection establishes the facts, and after root removal with hydro jetting or sewer line cleaning you can confirm the pipe is sound enough for lining.

Who owns which part of the sewer line

In Coachella the City Utilities Department runs the sewer main under the street. The private lateral between your house and that main is the owner's. A repair on your side needs a plumber, and work near the street may involve the City and a permit.

Parts of the east valley, including Thermal, still rely on septic systems. The Coachella Valley Water District is studying a sewer consolidation project for about 150 septic systems in east valley communities. If you are in a septic area, the repair path is different, so tell the dispatcher your location. Plumbing work in California is checked against the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Pipe material, slope and cleanout access fall under it, and a permit inspection is where an inspector looks at them.

What sewer line repair costs

Sewer work ranges widely, so every quote starts with a camera look.

Depth and lengthDeeper, longer repairs cost more.
MethodLining, spot digging and replacement are different scopes.
Surface restorationConcrete, landscaping and driveways affect cost.
PermitsStreet or utility work may require City permits.
