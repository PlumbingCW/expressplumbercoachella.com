Summer heat and how it breaks plumbing in Thermal

Summer afternoons in the east valley regularly climb past one hundred degrees. Exposed outdoor piping, hose bibs and heaters in sun-baked sheds take a beating. Plastic fittings become brittle, and a water heater in an uninsulated shed works harder than one indoors.

Inspect exposed lines each spring, insulate hot pipes where you can, and keep the heater's relief valve discharge clear. A water heater repair visit in May is cheaper than an emergency in July.

Water quality and septic in the east valley

Naturally occurring arsenic in groundwater has been a documented problem for some small east valley water systems. In March 2022 the EPA issued emergency orders to four mobile home parks on the Torres Martinez reservation in Thermal, citing arsenic above the 10 parts per billion limit. If you are on a well or small system, a lab test is the right first step, and a plumber installs treatment based on the result. See softener and filtration for what we install.

Many east valley homes also use septic. The Coachella Valley Water District has studied a sewer consolidation project that would replace roughly 150 septic systems in east valley communities. Until that happens, a backup at a septic home needs a different response, so tell us what you have.

Source types: EPA news release (March 2022) and CEQA notice for the East Coachella Valley Sewer Consolidation Planning Project.
