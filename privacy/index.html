What this policy covers

This policy explains how Coachella Express Plumbing handles information when you visit this website, call our line or email us. We keep the website simple. It has no forms and no accounts.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we ask for your name, address and a description of the problem so that we can schedule a plumber. We use this to book and complete your service and to improve how we answer calls.

Information we collect

We collect what you tell us on the phone or by email, such as your name, phone number, address and the plumbing problem. We may also collect basic technical data from your visit, such as browser type and pages viewed, through standard server logs and analytics tools.

How we use it

We use your information to schedule and complete service, to answer questions and to keep this website working. We share what is needed with the plumber assigned to your job. We do not use your information for unrelated purposes.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies or similar tools to measure traffic and improve pages. You can block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them does not affect your ability to call us.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, subject to any records we must keep. Email contact@expressplumbercoachella.com or call (760) 986-1999.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
