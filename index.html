Right here in Coachella

Emergency plumbing for homes and businesses near Pueblo Viejo and beyond

Coachella runs from the historic downtown district of Pueblo Viejo, near Sixth Street and Grapefruit Boulevard, out to newer subdivisions east and south. Older blocks near the original grid tend to have older drains and supply lines, which is why slow drains and pinhole leaks lead the calls there. Newer streets lean toward water heater and fixture problems.

Highway 111, which locals know as Grapefruit Boulevard, and Highway 86S are the main routes we use, with Interstate 10 just north of the city. If a crash or festival traffic slows those roads, tell the dispatcher your cross streets and we give you a realistic arrival window instead of a promise. Commercial streets like Cesar Chavez Street and Harrison Street have restaurants, retail centers and industrial lots with floor drains, so we dispatch for business calls there too.

Bagdouma Park and the neighborhoods around it sit in the middle of town, and a 24/7 emergency plumber can reach most Coachella addresses in a short drive. For a big job, we plan the visit by phone first so the plumber arrives with the right parts.

City resources worth knowing

The City of Coachella Utilities Department runs the sewer and lists a standby number, (760) 625-6781, for overflows. Call it first if your whole street is backing up, since the blockage may be in the main. The Building Division handles plumbing permits and is open Monday through Thursday.

Knowing who to call saves time in an emergency. If the problem is in your own line, call us. If it is in the City main, the standby line is the right number, and we can still help with any cleanup inside the house.
