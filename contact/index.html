What to tell the dispatcher

Give your address and cross streets, what the problem is and what you have already done. If water is spreading, say so right away. The dispatcher will tell you which valve to close and which breakers to switch off. If you know where the main shutoff is, mention it.

For commercial calls, add the business name and who can approve the price. For rentals, tell us whether the tenant or owner is on site.

Emergencies and planned jobs

Emergency problems such as burst pipes, sewage backups and failed water heaters go straight to the 24/7 line. Planned jobs like repipes, installs and inspections are scheduled by phone as well. Either way, you hear the price before work starts.

We cannot take bookings through a form. A short call is faster, and it lets the dispatcher ask the questions that matter.
