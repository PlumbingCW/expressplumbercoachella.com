Sewer camera inspection in Coachella, CA

A sewer camera inspection feeds a waterproof camera on a flexible rod through a cleanout and records the inside of the pipe. The video shows roots, cracks, offsets, belly sections, scale and blockages. It replaces guessing, which is the cause of many unnecessary repairs.

The inspection is a planned service. If your drains are backing up right now, see sewer line cleaning first, because the camera cannot see through standing sewage.

When a camera inspection pays for itself

  • The same drain has clogged more than once in a year
  • You are buying a house and want to know the lateral's condition
  • A contractor recommends replacement and you want proof
  • After cleaning or jetting, to confirm the pipe is intact
  • Roots or sinkholes near the sewer path
  • Planning remodel work that adds fixtures to the line

The video is yours to keep, which is the point. A recording lets you get a second opinion without paying for a second inspection.

What the technician looks for

The technician notes pipe material, joints, slope and any defects, with distance markers so a repair can be located. Some trucks include a locator that finds the camera head from above ground, which helps pinpoint where to dig.

Findings fall into three groups. Clean and sound pipe needs nothing. Pipe with roots or buildup needs hydro jetting or cleaning. Pipe that is cracked, offset or collapsed needs repair.

Reading the video with your plumber

Ask the technician to pause on every defect and state its distance from the cleanout. A root intrusion twelve feet out is a different job from one under the street, and the footage lets you compare quotes on the same facts. If one company proposes full replacement and the video shows a single offset joint, you have grounds to ask why.

Keep the file. Lenders, insurers and future buyers sometimes ask for it, and a dated recording of a sound line is a useful record of the home.

Buying or selling in Coachella

Coachella has older homes near the original city grid and many newer subdivisions. Pipe material and age differ. If you are buying an older house, a sewer scope helps you negotiate. If you are selling, a clean video can help buyers trust the home.

The lateral from the house to the City main is private property. The City's Utilities Department handles the main, and its sanitary superintendent line is listed on the City's site.
