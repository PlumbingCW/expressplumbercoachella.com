Emergency hydro jetting for stubborn backups

Hydro jetting in Coachella, CA is a method of cleaning a pipe with a hose and nozzle that sends high-pressure water in several directions, scouring the pipe wall instead of poking a hole through the blockage. It is the better answer when a snake has already failed, when a line clogs over and over, or when a backup is caused by grease and mineral scale. Our 24/7 line is open for backups at any hour.

While you wait, stop running water, especially washing machines and dishwashers, and keep people out of the lowest bathroom. Never pour chemical openers into a standing backup.

When jetting is the right call

Grease-coated lines

A cable opens a channel, jetting removes the coating that caused the clog.

Repeat main line clogs

Water pressure cleans the full circumference so the line stays open longer.

Root intrusion

Specialty nozzles cut fine roots but a camera should confirm the pipe is intact.

Commercial kitchens

Shops and restaurants that put grease down drains need regular jetting. See commercial plumbing.

What happens during a jetting visit

A technician inspects the line, often with a camera, to check for breaks, belly sections or offsets. Jetting a broken pipe can wash out the soil around it, so inspection comes first when the pipe condition is unknown. A sewer camera inspection before and after shows the result.

The jetting hose is fed through a cleanout and works downstream. Water pressure and nozzle type are matched to pipe size and material. Afterward, the line is flushed and checked with a camera. If the camera shows damage, a sewer line repair may be a better fit than more cleaning.

Why jetting matters in this area

Water in the Coachella Valley is drawn from groundwater, and mineral scale builds inside older pipes over time. In older parts of the city near Pueblo Viejo, the original sewer laterals may be clay or cast iron, which scales and roots more than modern plastic. Newer subdivisions use plastic pipe, which stays smoother.

The City's Utilities Department runs the sewer main. If your whole street is backing up, call the City's sewer standby line at (760) 625-6781 first, since jetting your line will not fix a blocked main.

What to do before we arrive

These steps limit damage and keep you safe until a plumber arrives.

  • Stop using water, especially washers and dishwashers
  • Keep people and pets out of the room with the backed-up drain
  • Do not pour chemical openers into a standing backup
  • Avoid skin contact with sewage and wash hands thoroughly if you touch it
  • Tell the dispatcher where your cleanout is, if you know
  • Move rugs and belongings off the floor near the drain
