What we do

Coachella Express Plumbing is a dispatch line for plumbing in Coachella, Indio, La Quinta, Thermal and the small communities around them. You call, a dispatcher listens to the problem, and a plumber is scheduled for the job. The line is answered 24/7 so a burst pipe at midnight gets the same attention as a dripping faucet at noon.

We keep the process simple on purpose. There are no online forms, no sign-up and no subscription. You describe the problem by phone, you hear the price before work starts, and you decide.

If you want to know who will do the work, ask when you call. A fair question gets a direct answer, and you are free to compare it with the contractor's license record before you agree to anything.

How we work

Every call begins with safety. The dispatcher tells you what to shut off, what to avoid and how to limit damage while a plumber is on the way. Then comes the price discussion. Prices depend on the job, so we explain what affects the number instead of guessing. After the visit, you get the details in writing.

We list what we can and cannot do on each service page. If a job sits outside what we handle, such as a City main line blockage, we say so and point you to the right office.

Checking any plumber's license

California licenses plumbing contractors through the Contractors State License Board. Its website lets you look up a license by number or business name and see whether it is active, with complaint disclosure. Use it on any contractor you plan to hire, including the ones we send.

Ask the plumber who arrives to show a license and confirm the company name on the paperwork. A reputable contractor expects the question and answers it plainly.

Rooted in the Coachella Valley

The east valley has its own plumbing quirks. Water comes from groundwater, so scale builds up inside heaters and fixtures. Some rural properties around Thermal use wells and septic systems instead of City utilities. Summer heat is hard on outdoor pipes and garage heaters. We write our service pages around those facts and cite official sources for utilities and permits.

There is no walk-in office. We dispatch to you across the valley, and we tell you what to expect when you call.

Source types: City of Coachella pages and California Contractors State License Board license check page.

What we handle and what we send elsewhere

We handle residential and light commercial plumbing: water heaters, drains, leaks, sewer lines, fixtures, repipes, installs and water treatment. We do not handle City main line blockages, which belong to the Utilities Department, or septic tank pumping, which needs a licensed pumper. When a call falls outside our work, the dispatcher says so and points you to the right office.

That honesty saves you time. A caller whose whole street is backing up gets the sewer standby number first and a plumber second. A homeowner on a well gets advice about testing before anyone sells them equipment. We would rather lose a job than send a plumber to a problem we cannot fix.

What to expect on the day

The plumber confirms the problem, explains the repair and gives the price before starting. If something unexpected turns up once a wall is open or a drain is scoped, you hear about it before more work is done. After the repair, the plumber tests the fix, cleans the work area and leaves you with the details in writing.

If you are not home, tell the dispatcher who will let the plumber in. We do not enter a property without someone's authorization, and we ask for a contact number we can reach during the visit.
