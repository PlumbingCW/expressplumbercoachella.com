When a clogged drain becomes an emergency

Drain cleaning in Coachella, CA means removing the blockage that stops water from leaving a fixture or your home, using a hand auger, a cable machine or high-pressure water. A clog turns urgent the moment water rises toward the rim, a toilet and a shower gurgle together, or sewage shows up in the lowest tub or floor drain. Our emergency line is open 24/7, including nights and holidays.

While you wait for the plumber, stop running water in the house, because every gallon you add has nowhere to go. Skip chemical drain openers. They can burn skin, harden into the trap and make the technician's job riskier. Move rugs and electronics off the floor near the affected drain and keep children and pets out of any standing water.

Where the clog usually sits

Which fixtures are slow tells a technician where to start. One slow drain points at that fixture's trap or branch line. Several slow drains point at the main line.

Kitchen sink

Grease, coffee grounds and food scraps cool and stick inside a 1.5 to 2 inch line. Disposals push the mess further down the pipe, they do not remove it.

Bathroom sink and tub

Hair wraps around the stopper and soap scum narrows the pipe. Most of these clear with a hand auger from the drain opening.

Toilet or floor drain

A toilet that clogs alone is a fixture problem. A toilet, shower and floor drain that clog together mean the branch or main line is the issue.

Main line

Every drain is slow, the toilet bubbles when the washer drains, or the lowest fixture backs up first. The cleanout outside the house is where a cable machine usually enters.

Snake, cable or jet: how the right method gets picked

A hand snake or drum auger handles most fixture clogs. A larger cable machine with a cutting head handles roots and compacted debris in the main line. When grease or mineral scale has coated the pipe wall, a plain cable only punches a hole, and hydro jetting is the better choice because water pressure scours the full pipe circumference.

If the same drain clogs again within weeks, a camera is cheaper than a third snaking. A sewer camera inspection shows whether the cause is a belly in the pipe, a cracked joint or roots, and that decides whether you need cleaning or a sewer line repair.

What matters about drains in Coachella homes

Sewer service in the city of Coachella is run by the City's Utilities Department, listed as the Coachella Water Authority and Sanitary District. Your pipe from the house to the street is yours to maintain, and the main under the street is the City's. That split decides who fixes a backup.

If your neighbors on the same street also have backups, the blockage may be in the City main, and the Utilities Department lists a sewer emergency standby line at (760) 625-6781. If only your house is affected, the stoppage is almost always on your side, and we can clear it. In dry desert soil, roots search hard for the moisture inside sewer joints, which is why older neighborhoods near Pueblo Viejo tend to see repeat main line clogs more than newer subdivisions.
